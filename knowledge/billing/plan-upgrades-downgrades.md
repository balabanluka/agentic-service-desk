# Plan upgrades and downgrades

Harborlight Workspace has three standard plans: **Starter**, **Growth**, and
**Scale**. Plans determine feature availability, included billable seats, API
capacity, and support level. Owners and Billing Admins can request plan changes;
Admins, Members, and Viewers cannot.

## Plan summary

| Plan | Included billable seats | Intended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | 3 | Small teams beginning with one workspace. |
| Growth | 10 | Teams that need expanded collaboration and integrations. |
| Scale | 25 | Larger teams with higher API and export capacity. |

Viewer accounts are not billable seats. Owners, Admins, and Members are
billable; details are in the seat-based charges policy.

## Upgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately after confirmation. Newly available features
and higher plan limits are applied at once. Harborlight creates a prorated charge
for the remaining time in the current billing period. For example, moving from
Starter to Growth halfway through a monthly period charges roughly half of the
difference between the two plan prices, plus any applicable additional seats.

The prorated charge appears as an open invoice or, for an eligible default card,
is collected automatically. If it cannot be collected, the normal failed-payment
schedule applies. An upgrade does not reset the workspace’s billing anchor date.

## Downgrades

A downgrade is scheduled for the next renewal date; it does not take effect
immediately and does not create a refund for unused current-plan time. This keeps
access predictable and avoids removing a feature in the middle of active work.

Before a downgrade is saved, Harborlight checks the target plan’s limits. The
Owner must resolve any overage first. Examples include reducing billable seats,
bringing export use within the target plan’s limits, or removing features that
are not available on the target plan. The workspace remains on the current plan
until the renewal date.

## Cancellation

Cancellation is a special scheduled downgrade to no paid plan. It takes effect
at the next renewal date unless required local law provides otherwise. The
workspace becomes read-only for 30 days after cancellation, allowing an Owner to
export permitted data. Cancellation does not erase paid invoices or ticket
history.

## Troubleshooting

If a plan change button is unavailable, check that the user is an Owner or
Billing Admin, that the workspace is not billing-restricted, and that there is
no already scheduled downgrade. If the invoice amount is unexpected, compare the
effective date, remaining billing-period days, current billable-seat count, and
any account credit before contacting Billing.
